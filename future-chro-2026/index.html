3 reasons why aspiring CHROs cannot afford to miss this exclusive session

  1. Think beyond HR matters

    Stop being left out of the CEO’s big decisions.

    Learn how to move beyond HR matters, earn a voice in critical business conversations, and become the strategic advisor your CEO turns to before making major decisions.

  2. Earn the trust of CEOs

    Earn your seat at the boardroom table — and the respect that comes with it.

    Discover what CEOs and board members truly value, how to speak their commercial language, and how to build the trust and credibility to influence decisions at the highest level.

  3. Lead with commercial credibility

    Why stop at CHRO when you could be the next CEO?

    Break the perception that HR leaders can only lead HR. Learn how to develop the commercial mindset, business acumen and leadership presence to position yourself as a credible future CEO candidate.

Kept to around 20 high-potential HR leaders, so every guest gets real time with Datuk Hishamudin Hasan, a former CEO who knows what it takes to earn trust, influence decisions and lead businesses.
